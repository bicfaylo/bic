Dynamic Web Systems & Dashboards

A database-driven system your team and customers log in to and use every day.

Price rangeTSh 2,500,000 – 5,000,000
What's included
  • Everything in Website Design
  • Database design (PostgreSQL or MySQL)
  • User accounts with roles, such as admin, staff and customer
  • Admin dashboard with reports and data you can export
  • Workflows such as registration, orders, bookings or applications
  • Integrations where needed: payments, SMS or email notifications, QR codes
  • Deployment, training for your team and handover
What sets the price

The number of user roles, the features and workflows, any payment or SMS integrations, and the reports you need. Simple systems sit near TSh 2.5M; larger multi-role platforms move towards TSh 5M.

Examples of my work

Field Link (student placements), Chimbo Mkononi (agent-based commerce) and the MUHAS conference event system (registration, QR name tags and check-in).

Hardware Prototyping

A working IoT or embedded prototype, from idea to a device you can test and demo.

Starting fromTSh 500,000
What's included
  • Requirements and component selection
  • Circuit design and prototyping on Arduino, ESP32 or STM32
  • Firmware for sensors, displays, motors and other modules
  • Connectivity where needed: Wi-Fi, GSM or LoRa
  • Optional dashboard to monitor the device remotely
  • Testing, demo and documentation
What sets the price

The number of sensors and modules, the connectivity, the enclosure, and whether you need a monitoring dashboard. Component costs are agreed with you up front.

Example of my work

Tone Salama: an STM32 water purification and vending unit with GSM telemetry, now deployed as 26 units across 4 regions. It won Innovation of the Year at Mzumbe University Innovation Day 2025.
